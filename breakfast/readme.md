# Hainanese Kaya Toast

## Description

A kopitiam breakfast classic from Singapore and Malaysia, popularized by Hainanese immigrants who ran the region's early coffee shops. Crisp toast is layered with caramel-tinged pandan coconut jam (kaya) and a cold slab of butter, served with soft boiled eggs and strong coffee.

### Ingredients:

**Kaya (Hainanese brown style)**
- [ ] 50 g sugar, for the caramel
- [ ] 3 tbsp hot water
- [ ] 4 large eggs
- [ ] 150 g sugar
- [ ] Pinch of salt
- [ ] 200 ml coconut milk
- [ ] 4 pandan leaves, tied in a knot (or 1/2 tsp pandan extract)

**Toast**
- [ ] 8 thin slices white sandwich bread
- [ ] Cold salted butter, cut into thin slabs

**To serve (optional)**
- [ ] 4 eggs, for soft boiling
- [ ] Dark soy sauce
- [ ] Ground white pepper

### Steps for preparation:

1. Make the caramel: melt 50 g sugar in a small pan over medium heat, swirling, until deep amber. Carefully add the hot water (it will sputter) and stir until smooth. Set aside.
2. Whisk the 4 eggs, 150 g sugar, and salt until the sugar is mostly dissolved. Strain through a sieve to remove any stringy bits.
3. Set a heatproof bowl over a pot of barely simmering water. Add the coconut milk and pandan leaves and warm until hot but not boiling.
4. Slowly pour the egg mixture into the coconut milk while whisking, then stir in the caramel.
5. Cook over the simmering water, stirring constantly with a spatula and scraping the sides, for 30 to 45 minutes, until the kaya is thick enough to coat the back of a spoon. Keep the heat low so the eggs don't curdle.
6. Remove the pandan leaves. If the kaya looks lumpy, blend briefly or press through a sieve. Let cool; it thickens further as it chills. Store in a clean jar in the fridge for up to 1 week.
7. Toast the bread until crisp and golden, ideally over a grill or in a toaster. Trim off the crusts.
8. Spread a generous layer of kaya on one slice, lay a slab of cold butter on top, and close with a second slice. Cut in half and serve immediately.
9. For the soft boiled eggs: place eggs in a bowl, cover with just boiled water, cover the bowl, and let sit 7 minutes. Crack into a small dish and season with dark soy sauce and white pepper. Dip the toast into the eggs.

### Notes:

- Short on time? Skip the caramel for a green, pandan forward Nyonya style kaya.
- Store bought kaya works fine for the toast; look for it in Asian grocery stores.
- Traditional pairing is kopi (coffee with condensed milk) or teh (tea with condensed milk).
- For an extra crisp version, toast thick slices, then split each one horizontally to make two thin slices.
