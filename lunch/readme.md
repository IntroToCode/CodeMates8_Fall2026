# Carne Asada Burrito

## Description

A Mission-style burrito built around carne asada, the grilled, citrus-marinated steak at the heart of northern Mexican cooking. Charred, juicy steak is wrapped in a warm flour tortilla with seasoned rice, refried beans, cheese, and a bright pico de gallo for a hearty, handheld meal.

### Ingredients:

**Steak and marinade**
- [ ] 600 g flank or skirt steak
- [ ] 3 tbsp fresh lime juice
- [ ] 2 tbsp fresh orange juice
- [ ] 2 tbsp neutral oil
- [ ] 3 garlic cloves, minced
- [ ] 1 jalapeño, minced
- [ ] 1 tsp ground cumin
- [ ] 1 tsp chili powder
- [ ] 1 tsp salt
- [ ] 1/2 tsp black pepper
- [ ] 1/4 cup cilantro, chopped

**Rice**
- [ ] 1 tbsp neutral oil
- [ ] 1 cup long-grain white rice
- [ ] 1/2 small onion, finely diced
- [ ] 2 tbsp tomato paste
- [ ] 2 cups chicken stock
- [ ] 1/2 tsp salt

**Pico de gallo**
- [ ] 2 Roma tomatoes, diced
- [ ] 1/4 white onion, finely diced
- [ ] 1/2 jalapeño, minced
- [ ] 2 tbsp cilantro, chopped
- [ ] 1 tbsp lime juice
- [ ] Pinch of salt

**To assemble**
- [ ] 4 large (30 cm) flour tortillas
- [ ] 1 can (400 g) refried beans, warmed
- [ ] 150 g Monterey Jack or cheddar, shredded
- [ ] 1/2 cup sour cream
- [ ] 1 avocado, sliced, or 1/2 cup guacamole

### Steps for preparation:

1. Marinate the steak: whisk together the lime juice, orange juice, oil, garlic, jalapeño, cumin, chili powder, salt, pepper, and cilantro. Coat the steak and marinate at least 30 minutes (up to 4 hours in the fridge).
2. Make the rice: heat the oil in a saucepan over medium heat. Add the rice and onion and stir about 3 minutes, until the rice turns lightly golden. Stir in the tomato paste, then add the stock and salt. Bring to a boil, cover, reduce to low, and cook 18 minutes. Rest covered 5 minutes, then fluff.
3. Make the pico de gallo: combine the tomatoes, onion, jalapeño, cilantro, lime juice, and salt. Set aside.
4. Cook the steak: heat a grill or cast-iron skillet over high heat until smoking. Remove the steak from the marinade, pat off excess, and sear 3 to 4 minutes per side for medium. Rest 5 minutes, then slice thinly against the grain and chop into bite-sized pieces.
5. Warm the tortillas: heat each tortilla in a dry skillet or directly over a gas flame about 15 seconds per side, until soft and pliable.
6. Assemble: spread a line of refried beans across the lower third of each tortilla. Top with rice, steak, cheese, pico de gallo, sour cream, and avocado. Don't overfill.
7. Roll: fold the bottom edge over the filling, tuck in the sides, and roll tightly away from you. Optionally sear seam-side down in a hot skillet for 1 minute to seal and crisp.

### Notes:

- Slicing against the grain is the key to tender carne asada; look for the lines in the meat and cut across them.
- Don't marinate longer than 4 hours; the citrus starts to make the texture mushy.
- Warm tortillas are essential; cold ones crack when you roll them.
- For a smaller, less filling version, skip the rice and double the pico de gallo.
- Swap the steak for grilled chicken thighs (cook 5 to 6 minutes per side) or seasoned black beans for a vegetarian version.
- Wrap leftover burritos in foil and reheat in a 180°C (350°F) oven for 15 to 20 minutes.
