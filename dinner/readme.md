# Table Nachos

## Description

A party-style spread where nachos are layered directly onto a parchment-lined table so everyone can gather around and dig in. Crispy tortilla chips are piled with seasoned ground beef, black beans, a warm cheese sauce, and fresh toppings for a shareable, no-plates-needed dinner that's perfect for game nights and big groups.

### Ingredients:

**Beverage**
- [ ] Coke Zero

**Seasoned beef**
- [ ] 1800 g ground beef (80/20)
- [ ] 2 tbsp neutral oil
- [ ] 2 medium onions, finely diced
- [ ] 6 garlic cloves, minced (or 1 tbsp jarred minced garlic)
- [ ] 4 tbsp chili powder
- [ ] 4 tsp ground cumin
- [ ] 2 tsp smoked paprika
- [ ] 2 tsp dried oregano
- [ ] 3 tsp salt
- [ ] 1 cup water

**Cheese sauce**
- [ ] 4 tbsp butter
- [ ] 4 tbsp all-purpose flour
- [ ] 4 cups whole milk
- [ ] 600 g sharp cheddar, shredded
- [ ] 300 g Monterey Jack, shredded
- [ ] 2 cans (115 g each) diced green chiles
- [ ] 1 tsp salt

**Base and beans**
- [ ] 4 large bags (about 1600 g total) sturdy tortilla chips
- [ ] 4 cans (400 g each) black beans, drained and rinsed
- [ ] 400 g Monterey Jack, shredded (for melting on top)

**Toppings**
- [ ] 6 Roma tomatoes, diced
- [ ] 2 small red onions, finely diced
- [ ] 2 cups pickled jalapeño slices
- [ ] 4 avocados, diced, or 4 cups guacamole
- [ ] 2 cups sour cream
- [ ] 2 cups salsa
- [ ] 8 scallions, thinly sliced
- [ ] 1 cup cilantro, chopped
- [ ] 4 limes, cut into wedges

**Equipment**
- [ ] Food-safe parchment paper or butcher paper to cover the table

### Steps for preparation:

1. Prep the table: wipe the table clean and dry, then cover it with overlapping sheets of parchment or butcher paper, taping the edges underneath so it doesn't slide.
2. Cook the beef: heat the oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Add the onion and cook 3 minutes until soft, then add the garlic for 30 seconds. Add the beef, breaking it up, and cook 6 to 8 minutes until browned. Drain excess fat.
3. Season the beef: stir in the chili powder, cumin, paprika, oregano, salt, and water. Simmer 3 to 4 minutes until the liquid is mostly absorbed. Keep warm.
4. Warm the beans: heat the black beans in a small saucepan with a pinch of salt over low heat.
5. Make the cheese sauce: melt the butter in a saucepan over medium heat, whisk in the flour, and cook 1 minute. Slowly whisk in the milk and simmer 3 to 4 minutes until thickened. Lower the heat, stir in the cheddar and Monterey Jack a handful at a time until smooth, then stir in the green chiles and salt.
6. Crisp the chips: spread the chips on baking sheets, sprinkle with the 400 g shredded Monterey Jack, and bake at 200°C (400°F) for 5 to 7 minutes, until the cheese melts.
7. Build the table: pour the cheesy chips onto the center of the paper in a long, even layer. Spoon the beef and beans over the top, then drizzle generously with the warm cheese sauce.
8. Top and serve: scatter the tomatoes, red onion, jalapeños, avocado, scallions, and cilantro over everything. Add dollops of sour cream and salsa, place lime wedges around the edges, and serve immediately.

### Notes:

- Use thick, restaurant-style chips; thin chips go soggy under the toppings.
- Build the nachos right before guests eat. Cook all components ahead and keep them warm, then assemble in under 5 minutes.
- Bake the chips in batches if needed; melting cheese onto them first keeps them crisp and helps toppings stick.
- Keep extra cheese sauce warm on the stove to refresh the nachos as people eat.
- For a vegetarian version, replace the beef with two more cans of black beans and 4 cups of roasted corn.
- For a smaller crowd, halve the recipe and build it on a large sheet pan instead of the table.
